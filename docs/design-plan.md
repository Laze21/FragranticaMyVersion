## 1. Principles and the big moves

### Principles, in our words

1. **The object first.** A fragrance is a thing on a counter. Every surface that shows a bottle shows it at its real proportions, standing on a floor, lit from one side, with a shadow. Nothing floats in a grey box.
2. **Ten seconds, then twenty minutes.** The first screen of a fragrance page answers six questions in plain words. Everything below is for the person who stays, and it is shaped, not stacked: each section has its own form, width and interval.
3. **The Trail is the mark.** One geometry (time × projection × character) drawn at every size, with one ruler, one palette and one nose. It appears where the eye lands first and is rare enough elsewhere to stay recognisable.
4. **Two voices, cleanly split.** Newsreader roman is the display and reading voice (titles, section heads, ledes, prose). Newsreader italic means exactly one thing: a fragrance name. Archivo is the instrument: labels, data, buttons, nav, numbers.
5. **Colour that means something.** Porcelain and ink are the house. One scent accent per fragrance, derived at fixed lightness so every page is tinted and none is loud. Oxblood, bergamot and juniper keep their fixed meanings (attention/you/owned).
6. **Honest once, not three times.** Demo data is labelled in the hero, in the sources table and in the banner, and nowhere else per page. Sample size is the brightest small text in a section, not the faintest.
7. **Quiet, with four signatures.** The atomizer press, the cap lift and spray, the Trail drawing on, and the shelf placement are the motion budget. Nothing grows on mount, nothing zooms on hover.
8. **Phones are designed, not shrunk.** One bottom bar, a header that gets out of the way, bottom sheets for everything dense, and a fragrance page under 5,500px.

### The four moves that change how the site feels

**Move 1. Reference-accurate bottles, physically lit, animated.** The client's own words: look up how the fragrances look, then make real, accurate, animated renders. Every one of the 50 bottles gets a per-bottle spec written from reference photography (silhouette, proportions, glass, liquid, cap, collar, label geometry), rendered with physical glass and a studio rig via difference-matting to a true-alpha poster, output as the existing three layers plus a floor reflection. Because the spec *is* the model, every one of the 50 also gets a GLB exported from the same spec with `open` and `spray` clips (the pipeline already exports GLBs); the eight flagships are hand-tuned first and gate the rest. 3D stays progressive enhancement behind `autoLoad3d()`; the page never waits for it. Acceptance: an enthusiast names the bottle at 200px without reading the label; three heroes are visibly different materials. (WP1)

**Move 2. Invert the typography and remove the boxes.** Newsreader roman takes every title and section head; the wide-bold grotesque leaves. Card plates lose their tinted box and become bottles on a shared floor at real scale; the five "paper sidebar" boxes become ruled typographic asides; the footer becomes a colophon. This single change moves the site from "startup" to "publication". (WP0, WP3)

**Move 3. Make the per-fragrance colour arrive.** The scent-accent cascade bug means every page today is the same grey. Fix the cascade, derive washes in OKLCH at fixed lightness, re-cut the Trail palette for separation, and let the hero stage, data marks and note bands carry each fragrance's hue. Three heroes side by side must differ. (WP0, WP2)

**Move 4. Reshape the fragrance page.** Full-width Trail directly under the hero; a sticky rail that is the object's spine (thumb, score, shelf actions, section list, one contextual vote prompt); sections that differ in width, surface and interval; seeded reviews; and one 60px bar on phones. (WP4, WP5, WP12)

WP1 does **not** edit `scripts/generate-seed.ts`; it writes the manifest and WP12's generator reads it (contract below).
Steps:
1. Reference pass, per bottle: open the house's product page and press imagery (looking is allowed; copying image files or CAD is not; no competitor databases). Record in a spec sheet: silhouette family, height/width/depth in cm from the house or retailer spec, shoulder and heel profile, glass finish/tint/gradient, liquid colour and fill, cap kind/material/proportions, collar, label geometry (house and name in a neutral engraved sans or embossed, never Newsreader), decor. Store `bottleReference: { sourceUrl, checkedAt, heightMm }` on the seed as provenance for the illustration. Where a house page cannot be reached from the build environment, use a retailer's spec for dimensions and the house's own press description for materials, record which, and leave `checkedAt` null so the Details page says "reference not yet checked" rather than pretending. Extend the v2 spec where a real bottle needs it (e.g. stepped shoulders, asymmetric heels, sequin/stud maps for Black Opium, the Sauvage magnetic cap band, N°5's faceted stopper proportions, Aventus's crest plate). These are original illustrations made from observation, the same way an editorial illustrator draws a product; several bottle shapes (N°5, Black Opium among them) are registered designs or 3D marks, so the handoff doc (WP13) lists them for a legal read before launch. That is a launch item, not a reason to draw them less accurately now.
2. Physical glass for posters: posters are rendered headless, so cost is irrelevant. `materials.ts` gains a `physical` mode: `MeshPhysicalMaterial` with `transmission 1`, `ior 1.5`, `thickness` 0.3–0.6 of body depth, `attenuationColor` = glass tint with per-bottle `attenuationDistance`; the liquid as its own mesh with its own absorption (N°5 amber #C8923A, not #F1E2B2) and a meniscus; dark bottles via attenuation, not opaque diffuse; cap materials with real roughness/metalness. True alpha with refraction baked: render each bottle twice, against black and against white, and solve per pixel `alpha = 1 − mean(W − B)`, `colour = B / alpha` (difference matting), so the poster composites over any wash without a halo. Studio: keep `studioEnvironment()`'s strip softboxes, add a floor plane for the contact shadow and a 12% floor reflection baked into `<slug>.shadow.webp`. Camera 35mm-equivalent 3/4 view with ±6° per-bottle yaw so a shelf is not a row of clones; frame so the bottle fills ≥ 85% of a 4:5 frame; export 1200px tall webp ≤ 120KB plus the existing body/cap/shadow layers and `layers.json` (nozzle, cap box). Blur placeholders (16px base64) generated here.
3. 3D, all 50, flagships first. The spec is the model, and `exportGlb` with `buildClips` already exists, so every bottle gets a GLB with the `open` and `spray` clips defined in section 4 (cap and pump as separate nodes; the clips are generated from the spec's cap box and nozzle, not hand-animated). The eight flagships are hand-tuned and gated first: `dior-sauvage` (exists; re-export with physical materials), `chanel-no-5`, `aventus`, `bleu-de-chanel-edp`, `black-opium`, `baccarat-rouge-540`, `coco-mademoiselle`, `acqua-di-gio`. After the gate, the remaining 42 are exported in one batch. Budget: ≤ 300KB per GLB after gltf-transform (meshopt; check the current stable version before adding the dependency), decals as 512px KTX2 or baked vertex colour, no textures above 1024. `has3d` on the seed goes away; `manifest.json` lists `model` per slug and `fragrance_assets.model_version` is `'2'` for every re-export. The live viewer uses transmission on capable devices (`deviceMemory ≥ 8`, no `low-power` hint, dpr ≤ 2) and the existing fresnel glass otherwise; both share `cameraFor`/`FRAMING` so the poster → 3D crossfade does not jump. Loading rules are unchanged: poster first, GLB only after `autoLoad3d()` says yes or the user asks, never on cards, never on phones under the save-data hint.
4. Acceptance per bottle: an enthusiast names it at 200px without reading the label; silhouette overlay against the reference within ~4% of the outline height; no site serif on the label; three heroes visibly different materials (Sauvage navy-to-clear gradient glass with a magnetic black cap and silver band, N°5 clear glass with see-through amber and an emerald-cut stopper, Aventus near-black glass with a tall silver cap and crest plate). The home feature's Black Opium must read as Black Opium (black studded glass, pink interior glow, not a brown rounded square). Where a reference cannot be matched, keep the "Illustration" tag honest.
Contract for other packages: `FragranceCard` reads `bottleHeightMm` and `blurData`; `BottleStage` reads `layers.json` as today; `scripts/generate-seed.ts` (WP12) reads `public/bottles/manifest.json` for `bottle_height_mm`, `blur_data` and the `fragrance_assets` model rows, and fails the build if a slug is missing from it.
