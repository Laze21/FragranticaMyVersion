## AI / "vibe-coded" UI tells (sources in the round-1 agent report; key ones)
- Indigo/purple→blue gradients (Adam Wathan's "bg-indigo-500" apology, Aug 2025, https://x.com/adamwathan/status/1953510802159219096).
- "Statistical average design": purple gradients, Inter, three boxes, rounded corners.
- shadcn defaults: `rounded-2xl shadow-lg backdrop-blur` everywhere; cards in cards; side-stripe accent borders; glassy sticky navbar.
- Emoji or Lucide icons in tinted rounded tiles above every heading; sparkles on anything "AI".
- Same fade-up on every section with 100ms stagger; hover lift on cards; ignoring reduced motion.
- **Second-wave "tasteful" defaults** (what models produce when told "less AI"): *warm cream + terracotta accent* (Claude's own UI colour),
  near-black + single acid accent, *all-caps monospace labels*, *01/02/03 numbered features*, *broadsheet hairline rules*, fake macOS window dots.
  (https://github.com/funboy322/avoid-ai-design, https://thomas-wiegold.com/blog/claude-code-frontend-design-plugin/)
- Copy: Elevate/Unlock/Seamless/Empower/Delve/Tapestry; rule-of-three slogans; "not just X, it's Y"; heavy em dashes; "Get Started".
- What works: deliberate type pairing with real contrast, fixed type scale tokens, 70/20/10 colour discipline with real imagery carrying colour,
  asymmetric grids, restrained & consistent radii, one meaningful motion moment, real content.

### Consequence for this project
The brief's starting palette (Porcelain #F4EFE6 + Oxblood) sits right next to the cream+terracotta trope, and "luxury fragrance" pushes
toward cream + Didot. We keep the brief's materials but change their *roles*: see `docs/04-design-system.md`.
